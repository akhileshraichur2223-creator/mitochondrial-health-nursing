01 / Evidence

From mitochondrial function
to better outcomes.

A narrative evidence synthesis connecting mitochondrial dysfunction with fatigue, chronic conditions, physical function and healthy ageing.

⚡

Why mitochondria matter

Mitochondria regulate cellular bioenergetics, calcium homeostasis, redox signalling and cell survival. The poster highlights their relevance to chronic disease, persistent fatigue and age-related functional decline.

See nursing pathway →
THEME 01

Dysfunction & fatigue

Evidence links mitochondrial dysfunction with fatigue, reduced physical function and poorer quality of life in chronic conditions.

THEME 02

Lifestyle support

Balanced nutrition, physical activity, sleep and stress management are highlighted as potential supports for mitochondrial function.

THEME 03

Nurse-led care

Education, behaviour counselling and individualized care planning may support fatigue management and functional outcomes.

Enhanced quality of life & healthy ageingperson-centred outcomes

Energy & fatigue

Assess fatigue and functional limitations; reinforce individualized education, symptom management and sustainable activity strategies.

Cognition & wellbeing

Use person-centred assessment and psychosocial support while considering how chronic conditions and ageing affect daily function.

Physical function

Support appropriate physical activity, nutrition and self-management strategies, with attention to mobility and healthy ageing.
